Lagotto is owned and run by Italian‑born Head Chef Matteo Fulchiati, whose seasonal, creative cooking anchors the kitchen, and British‑born Liam Cree, whose natural warmth and exceptional hospitality lead our front‑of‑house team.

Recognised with hats in The Age Good Food Guide for four years running — most recently described as an “Inner‑northern charmer exuding effortless, understated luxury.”

Lagotto was co‑founded by the late Katie McCormack, whose warmth, mentorship and genuine love of hospitality live on through the Katie McCormack Young Service Talent Award — a $10,000 prize and mentoring program for Victoria’s rising front‑of‑house talent, proudly supported by Milieu Hospitality and presented each year by The Age Good Food Guide.

It’s this same care — in the kitchen and on the floor — that keeps Lagotto feeling like a true neighbourhood table.
